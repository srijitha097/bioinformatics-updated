# Scientific limitations

**Phenotype ranking.** The 30 literature cases represent one case and one candidate allele per gene. This candidate panel is enriched for known diagnoses. Disease knowledge and case publications may overlap. Restricting annotations to explicit non-case PMIDs lowered Top-1 from 95.0% to 45.5% and MRR from 0.969 to 0.541. This restriction simultaneously changes annotation coverage and potential leakage. Seven causal genes lost every positive match. The difference cannot be attributed solely to leakage. Candidate genomes, unaffected controls, ancestry-specific frequency thresholds and within-gene allele discrimination were unavailable. HPO term deletion and addition are simulated perturbations. [Citation-restricted results](comparative_genomics/rare/distinct_publication.tsv)

**ACMG/AMP evidence.** The 19 reconstructions deliberately span reference classes. A versioned SCV may use different evidence strengths than a later ClinGen entry, as observed for GJB2 p.Met34Thr. Historical ENIGMA and CFTR2 records use their own frameworks. Published facts and awarded strengths were not independently replicated. Full OMIM access and submission-level individual genotypes were unavailable.

**Somatic tiers.** Twenty-three contexts cover 22 profiles and several biomarker classes. The sample is enriched for already accepted assertions. One paper per context undercaptures guidelines, negative results and later evidence. Some assessments concern fusion or mutation classes, not a single base change. The initial review is not a complete contemporary actionability classification. The post-comparison FDA review illustrates how the evidence scope changes conclusions. It is excluded from the initial comparison. None of these reports supplies individual treatment advice.

**Variant benchmarking.** Single public genome, older truth release, captured confident autosomal regions only. Exact normalized biallelic matching excludes equivalent complex haplotypes, multiallelic genotypes, and difficult regions outside the truth BED. REF verification and left alignment were checked for selected PASS biallelic capture records. They do not resolve haplotype equivalence. Depth filtering can assess only already-called query sites. The 30-gene subset contains no qualifying truth indels, making indel recall undefined.

**Copy number.** The synthetic development and test cohorts are distinct draws from one simulator. Linear GC correction benefits from a simulated log-depth trend that is linear in GC. Control selection, coverage scaling and segment thresholds were tuned jointly, so their individual causal contributions require matched ablations. In the real cell-line slice, the CCLE array segments supply an orthogonal comparison but lack a balanced set of independent losses, gains and neutral regions. Regional median centering can remove a broad gain. No real-data CNV accuracy or clinical diagnostic validation is claimed.

The original CNV holdout’s equal true-positive totals conceal different target identities. On three additional seeds the locked model had fewer true positives than the comparator. The rare-disease case PMID directly overlapped supporting HPO citations in 21 cases. [Final audit](FINAL_AUDIT.md)

Archived-input replay depends on software versions. samtools 1.21 produced slightly different HCC1143 depths from 1.25 using the same BAMs and options. A fresh download from mutable database endpoints can also change results. [Repository review](FINAL_GITHUB_REVIEW.md)
