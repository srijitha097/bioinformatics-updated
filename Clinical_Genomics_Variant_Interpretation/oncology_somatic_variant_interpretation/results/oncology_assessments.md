# Research-only oncology assessments

## ERBB2 Amplification — HER2 Positive Breast Cancer
- **Context** Predictive
- **Research tier** Tier I - Level A
- **Source tier** Tier I - Level A
- **Evidence summary** Randomized metastatic breast cancer study evaluated adding trastuzumab to chemotherapy in HER2-overexpressing tumors. The US label permits biomarker-selected use in defined breast cancer indications. The CIViC amplification profile requires appropriate HER2 assay context.
- **PMIDs** 11248153
- **Evidence links** https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/103792s5359lbl.pdf
- **CIViC assertion** https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/2

## v::ALK Fusion — Lung Non-small Cell Carcinoma
- **Context** Predictive
- **Research tier** Tier I - Level A
- **Source tier** Tier I - Level A
- **Evidence summary** Randomized evidence in previously treated ALK-positive advanced NSCLC showed progression-free survival benefit versus chemotherapy. The US label supports metastatic ALK-positive NSCLC. This assessment is scoped to ALK fusion-positive NSCLC and does not rank competing ALK inhibitors.
- **PMIDs** 23724913
- **Evidence links** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=2a51b0de-47d6-455e-a94c-d2c737b04ff7
- **CIViC assertion** https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/3

## EGFR L858R — Lung Non-small Cell Carcinoma
- **Context** Predictive
- **Research tier** Tier I - Level A
- **Source tier** Tier I - Level A
- **Evidence summary** LUX-Lung 3 tested first-line afatinib against chemotherapy in EGFR-mutated lung adenocarcinoma. L858R is a non-resistant sensitizing alteration covered in the metastatic NSCLC labeling context. Trial results from pooled common mutations do not estimate a variant-specific effect.
- **PMIDs** 23816960
- **Evidence links** https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fda-approves-first-generics-gilotrif-afatinib-tablets
- **CIViC assertion** https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/6

## BRAF V600E — Melanoma
- **Context** Predictive
- **Research tier** Tier I - Level A
- **Source tier** Tier I - Level A
- **Evidence summary** COMBI-v randomized patients with untreated BRAF V600E/K metastatic melanoma and showed improved survival for dabrafenib plus trametinib versus vemurafenib. The FDA labeling supports this combination in unresectable or metastatic BRAF V600E/K melanoma. The estimate pools V600E and V600K.
- **PMIDs** 25399551
- **Evidence links** https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/202806s013lbl.pdf
- **CIViC assertion** https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/7

## ACVR1 G328V — Diffuse Midline Glioma, H3 K27-altered
- **Context** Diagnostic
- **Research tier** Tier II - Level C
- **Source tier** Tier II - Level C
- **Evidence summary** Pediatric midline high-grade astrocytoma sequencing identified recurrent somatic ACVR1 mutations. G328V can be supportive context in the CIViC disease setting. The cohort establishes recurrence and does not establish ACVR1 G328V alone as a defining criterion for H3 K27-altered disease.
- **PMIDs** 24705250
- **Evidence links** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24705250/
- **CIViC assertion** https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/9

## ETV6::NTRK3 Fusion — B-lymphoblastic Leukemia/lymphoma
- **Context** Predictive
- **Research tier** Tier II - Level C
- **Source tier** Tier II - Level C
- **Evidence summary** Single-patient expanded-access evidence in adult Ph-like ALL with ETV6::NTRK3 demonstrated temporary cytoreduction. A single adult case gives limited durability and generalizability evidence. The tumor-agnostic solid tumor indication cannot be transferred to leukemia.
- **PMIDs** 31905241
- **Evidence links** https://www.cancer.gov/research/participate/clinical-trials-search/v?id=NCT03834961
- **CIViC assertion** https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/39
