# Molecular oncology evidence reviews

Research only. Tier and level describe the evidence examined. Selection used accepted CIViC contexts and linked studies. Some reference tiers had already been visible during phase one. Judgments were recorded separately before the comparison join.

## EGFR L858R in Lung Non-small Cell Carcinoma

Predictive · Erlotinib · defer. The cited paper emphasizes uncommon EGFR alterations. Its abstract does not establish L858R-specific erlotinib benefit. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21132006/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/5)

## v::NTRK1 Fusion in Solid Tumor

Predictive · Larotrectinib · II-D. One metastatic sarcoma with LMNA::NTRK1 regressed on LOXO-101. This case alone cannot estimate response across partner genes and tumor types. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26216294/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/8)

## BRAF V600E in Melanoma

Predictive · Vemurafenib,Cobimetinib · I-A. The phase 1b combination study suggested activity in BRAF V600 melanoma. The US cobimetinib label explicitly includes V600E with vemurafenib in unresectable or metastatic melanoma. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25037139/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/10)

## BRAF V600K in Melanoma

Predictive · Vemurafenib,Cobimetinib · I-A. The randomized coBRIM trial supports the combination in V600-mutant advanced melanoma. The FDA indication explicitly covers V600K, although the pooled estimate is not allele-specific. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27480103/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/11)

## BRAF V600E in Colorectal Cancer

Prognostic · Poor Outcome · II-C. Stage IV colorectal cancers with V600E had lower observed two-year survival than D594G and wild-type groups. A retrospective comparison is susceptible to treatment and disease-subtype differences. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27404270/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/20)

## HEY1::NCOA2 Fusion in Mesenchymal Chondrosarcoma

Diagnostic · Positive · II-C. Fusion FISH supported identification of mesenchymal chondrosarcoma in adequately prepared specimens. Inadequate samples lacked signal, limiting negative inference. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23252872/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/21)

## DNAJB1::PRKACA Fusion in Fibrolamellar Carcinoma

Diagnostic · Positive · II-C. A recurrent chimeric transcript was detected in 15 of 15 fibrolamellar tumors. A discovery cohort does not establish diagnostic specificity across all liver tumors. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24578576/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/24)

## FLT3 D835 in Acute Myeloid Leukemia

Predictive · Gilteritinib · II-D. Gilteritinib produced antileukemic activity in FLT3-mutant model systems. Model response alone gives limited clinical evidence for the D835 subgroup. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28516360/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/33)

## v::ALK Fusion in Lung Non-small Cell Carcinoma

Predictive · Alectinib · II-C. A single-arm phase 1/2 trial observed alectinib responses in ALK-rearranged advanced NSCLC. Lack of a randomized comparator limits this paper alone. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23639470/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/34)

## FLT3 ITD in Acute Myeloid Leukemia

Predictive · Gilteritinib · I-B. ADMIRAL found longer survival versus salvage chemotherapy in relapsed/refractory FLT3-mutated AML. The trial pooled FLT3 alterations, limiting inference about ITD-specific effect size. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31665578/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/38)

## SNX2::ABL1 Fusion in B-lymphoblastic Leukemia/lymphoma, BCR-ABL1–like

Diagnostic · Positive · defer. The Ph-like ALL study establishes a class of kinase lesions. The abstract lacks sufficient SNX2::ABL1-specific diagnostic discrimination. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25207766/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/43)

## KANK1::NTRK2 Fusion in Solid Tumor

Predictive · Larotrectinib · defer. The source title reports a pediatric NTRK2-fused tumor response. No abstract was available to confirm the exact partner or response assessment. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34651095/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/51)

## EML4::NTRK3 Fusion in Solid Tumor

Predictive · Entrectinib · defer. Two phase I trials showed activity in fusion-selected cancers. The abstract does not resolve the clinical effect for EML4::NTRK3 specifically. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28183697/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/52)

## ETV6::NTRK3 Fusion in Congenital Fibrosarcoma

Predictive · Larotrectinib · II-D. One infantile fibrosarcoma with the named fusion responded radiographically to LOXO-101. A single observation cannot quantify durability across the disease. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27093299/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/56)

## EWSR1::WT1 Fusion in Desmoplastic Small Round Cell Tumor

Diagnostic · Positive · II-C. Tumor breakpoint and transcript studies support a recurrent EWSR1::WT1 association. Application as a diagnostic marker requires integration with morphology. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7862627/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/76)

## CIC::DUX4 Fusion in CIC-rearranged Sarcoma

Diagnostic · Positive · II-C. CIC::DUX4 characterized a subset of EWSR1-negative primitive sarcomas. Selection of the study cohort limits specificity estimates. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22072439/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/79)

## BCOR ITD in Kidney Clear Cell Sarcoma

Diagnostic · Positive · II-C. BCOR internal tandem duplications recurred in childhood clear cell sarcoma of kidney. The molecular association supports a diagnostic workup within this histology. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26573325/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/80)

## KIT D816V in Systemic Mastocytosis

Predictive · Imatinib · I-A. The phase II study found little activity with KIT D816V. US imatinib labeling limits the aggressive systemic mastocytosis indication to absent or unknown D816V. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19193436/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/85)

## ZFTA::v Fusion in Supratentorial Ependymoma, ZFTA Fusion–positive

Diagnostic · Positive · II-C. A series of non-RELA ZFTA fusions supports rearrangement testing in supratentorial ependymomas. Generalization to every possible fusion partner is uncertain. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34389065/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/87)

## FOXR2 Rearrangement in CNS Neuroblastoma With FOXR2 Activation

Diagnostic · Positive · defer. Methylation-based reclassification identified CNS neuroblastoma with FOXR2 activation. The abstract does not validate rearrangement alone as a diagnostic test. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36895035/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/93)

## EWSR1::FLI1 Fusion in Ewing Sarcoma

Diagnostic · Positive · II-C. A round-cell tumor study compared FISH and RT-PCR. Transcript testing can identify the partner, whereas break-apart testing by itself cannot identify FLI1. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16258512/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/97)

## EWSR1::ERG Fusion in Ewing Sarcoma

Diagnostic · Positive · II-C. Integrated testing of suspected Ewing sarcoma identified rearrangements and fusion partners. RNA quality and primers affect detection of individual subtypes. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23706910/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/98)

## LMNA::NTRK1 Fusion in Lipofibromatosis-like Neural Tumor

Diagnostic · Positive · II-D. One subcutaneous tumor carrying the fusion showed atypical immunostaining. This observation supports a differential diagnosis with substantial uncertainty. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32299759/) · [CIViC context](https://civicdb.org/links/assertions/99)
